(M)oi!

Data developer during working hours, climber, gamer, and e-skater in the free time

I began my time at Aitomation Oy as a trainee, quickly familiarizing myself with the company’s customers and existing BI solutions. Early on, I contributed to customer projects, supporting the development and maintenance of Power BI reports. Alongside project work, I dedicated significant time strengthening my Power BI expertise, focusing on DAX, data modelling, and visualization best practices. I currently work as a Data Developer, designing, building and maintaining end-to-end BI solutions. I like to work closely with customers throughout the development cycle, ensuring solutions align with business needs and modern BI practices.

My academic background is in Environmental Sciences, where I am currently working towards a Doctor of Science degree through the Sustainable Use of Renewable Natural Resources Doctoral Programme at the University of Helsinki. I hold a Master of Science degree from the Forest Sciences and Business Programme from the same university and a Bachelor of Engineering in Forestry from the Federal University of Espirito Santo.
